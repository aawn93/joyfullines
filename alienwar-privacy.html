Alien War – Privacy Policy

Developer: Joyful Lines · Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Alien War (package com.joyfullines.alienwar) uses, and why. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information the game itself stores

The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location, and it has no sign-in or chat.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, hearts and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not upload it to our own servers. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for extra hearts. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, clicked or watched) and crash or performance diagnostics

This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

3. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the ad and Play services described above, we do not share information with anyone.

5. Children

Alien War is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security and retention

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through your device settings or directly with Google. You can always contact us for help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
